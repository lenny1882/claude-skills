- Narrative and commentary: how or when something came about, release-by-release history,
  what happened in a run, stories told to justify a rule, opinions about the owner or the
  work, and dates whose only purpose is to say when something was decided.
- Attributions of any kind ("as asked", "I chose", "the owner chose", a name with a date),
  reported speech, question-by-question logs and their answers, and what was proposed,
  offered, rejected or refused and by whom.
- A commit message that says what the commit did to the text instead of the behaviour it
  gives.
- A placeholder that the text around it gives back: its length, its spelling, what kind of
  word it is or what else it is used as, or an ordinary word replaced so that the sentence
  decodes it.
